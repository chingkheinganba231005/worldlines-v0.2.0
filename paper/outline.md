# WORLDLINE manuscript outline — research plan, not a results paper

1. Problem: distinguish reported-event displacement, forecast innovations and instrumentation change.
2. Related work: compositional analysis, prequential forecasting, distribution shift, event extraction, sequential testing, news-selection bias.
3. Data: lawful provenance, exact availability protocol, frozen extraction/representation, missingness and source coverage.
4. Method: separate measurements, coverage control channel, ILR forecasts, uncertainty, alert validation.
5. Evaluation: preregistered chronological split; audited consequential-event labels; matched false-alert budgets; simple baselines; source/language ablations.
6. Results: currently only synthetic controls and four-slot descriptive sample. Replace with held-out real-data estimates and uncertainty; disclose failures.
7. Limitations: partial observability, source independence, extraction and temporal leakage, calibration under drift, no causal identification.
8. Reproducibility: source commits, dataset manifests, seeds, release boundaries, computational cost.

Primary methodological starting points: Aitchison1982 compositional analysis; Dawid1984 prequential approach; Gneiting & Raftery2007 proper scoring rules; Feydy et al.2019 Sinkhorn divergences. Verify bibliographic details and conduct a current related-work review before claiming novelty.
